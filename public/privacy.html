Pick Predict

Privacy Policy

Pick Predict stores a Twitch-provided opaque viewer identifier, chosen display name, predictions, and scores to operate channel leaderboards. It does not receive Twitch passwords or email addresses. Broadcaster authentication uses Twitch OAuth and a signed session cookie.

Anonymous viewer identifiers are not treated as persistent identities. Recovery snapshots may contain opaque identifiers, display names, predictions, and scores. Broadcasters should store those files securely.

Data is used only to provide live predictions, leaderboards, moderation, recovery, and service security. It is not sold. To request deletion, contact the support address listed with the Twitch Extension.
